### What are you working on that makes you come alive? (< 200 words) -- DRAFT (195 words)

I'm building Mizan: open infrastructure for ethical, decentralised investing.

Most finance runs on trust you can't verify. Banks and states can freeze your money. "Halal" and "ethical" labels are vouched for by someone you have to believe. AI forecasts sound equally confident whether they're right or wrong.

Mizan replaces each with something checkable. It trades only spot crypto you actually own, so no single authority controls access. Islamic finance rules, drawn from a cited fatwa, are enforced in code and can't be switched off: no interest, no leverage, no derivatives, no market manipulation. Contested rulings are logged as open questions rather than quietly decided.

The prediction layer combines global event data, crowd-reaction simulation (MiroFish), time-series forecasting (TimesFM) and Inkling 2, a model trained to be calibrated about its own uncertainty. Nothing it predicts can move money until it has been tested against what actually happened, asset by asset.

Nearly two billion Muslims, and anyone who wants principled finance, are underserved by banks, crypto and overconfident AI. I want to give them a system whose ethics and predictions they can audit themselves.

The rules engine and backtester are built; I'm now extending them to crypto with a live demo.
